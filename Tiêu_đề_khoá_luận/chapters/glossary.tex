Dec-POMDP & Decentralised Partially Observable Markov Decision Process & Extension of MDP in which each agent receives only a local observation of the global state \\ \hline
DDQN  & Double Deep Q-Network & DQN variant that decouples action selection and value evaluation to reduce Q-value overestimation bias \\ \hline
DQN   & Deep Q-Network & Q-learning algorithm that uses a deep neural network to approximate the action-value function \\ \hline
DRL   & Deep Reinforcement Learning & Reinforcement learning using deep neural networks as function approximators \\ \hline
GAE   & Generalised Advantage Estimation & Advantage estimator used in PPO that interpolates between Monte Carlo returns and TD estimates \\ \hline
IDDQN & Independent Double Deep Q-Network & DDQN under an independent multi-agent architecture with parameter sharing across agents \\ \hline
IDQN  & Independent Deep Q-Network & DQN under an independent multi-agent architecture with parameter sharing across agents \\ \hline
IPPO  & Independent Proximal Policy Optimization & PPO under an independent multi-agent architecture with parameter sharing across agents \\ \hline
IQR   & Interquartile Range & Range between the 25th and 75th percentiles, used to measure statistical spread \\ \hline
MARL  & Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning & RL framework in which multiple agents simultaneously interact within a shared environment \\ \hline
MDP   & Markov Decision Process & Formal framework for modelling sequential decision-making under uncertainty \\ \hline
MLP   & Multi-Layer Perceptron & Feedforward neural network with one or more fully connected hidden layers \\ \hline
OD    & Origin-Destination & Matrix describing vehicle trip demand between each pair of origin and destination zones \\ \hline
PPO   & Proximal Policy Optimization & Policy-gradient algorithm that clips the policy update ratio to ensure stable, monotonic improvement \\ \hline
RL    & Reinforcement Learning & Machine learning paradigm in which an agent learns by interacting with an environment and receiving reward signals \\ \hline
SUMO  & Simulation of Urban MObility & Open-source microscopic, multi-modal traffic simulator developed by the German Aerospace Center \\ \hline
TraCI & Traffic Control Interface & SUMO programming interface for real-time external control of traffic signals and individual vehicles \\ \hline
